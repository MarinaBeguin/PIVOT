    Move an object

    Click an object, then click where it should go: under a table, on a desk (with "Carry it"), anywhere. After a move, the next click picks a new object; to change it before, use "Change", the list, Ctrl-click or Esc. Every move is planned and checked against the real geometry before it happens.
